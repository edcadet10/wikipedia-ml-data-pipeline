# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Critique claims, code, and experiments rather than people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts and respect privacy and content licenses.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), marking the report as a conduct issue. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or impose
a permanent ban according to severity and context. Reports will be handled as privately
as reasonably possible; conflicts of interest should be disclosed and recused.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
