# Governance

Jeff Cadet is the initial maintainer and final decision-maker for v0.x. Design decisions
should be made in public issues or discussions, with the relevant evidence, alternatives,
and claim boundary recorded before implementation.

Maintainers may merge a change after required checks pass and review concerns are
resolved. Compatibility, schema, licensing, security, and claim-scope changes require
explicit maintainer approval. Urgent security fixes may follow a private advisory and
be documented publicly after coordinated release.

Consistent, constructive contributors may be invited as reviewers or maintainers.
Maintainer status can be removed for inactivity, repeated policy violations, or loss of
project trust after a documented review. Governance changes are made by pull request.
